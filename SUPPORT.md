## Before you ask

Search [open and closed issues](https://github.com/phififofum/ope/issues?q=is%3Aissue) and
[Discussions](https://github.com/phififofum/ope/discussions). Closed issues are often the
answer, and they carry the reasoning with them.

## When you do ask

Include the command you ran, the whole error, your OS, your Python version, and what you
expected instead. A question with those five things usually gets answered the same day; a
question without them gets a request for them, which costs you a day.

## Response times

This is a volunteer project in design stage. Issues and discussions get a reply within a week.
Security reports get one within three working days. If something has gone quiet for longer,
a reply on the thread is a perfectly reasonable nudge.

## What we cannot help with

- Running the game — there is no playable build yet. See [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md).
- Godot itself — the [Godot docs](https://docs.godotengine.org/) and community are better at
  that than we are.
- Third-party mods. Report those to their authors.
